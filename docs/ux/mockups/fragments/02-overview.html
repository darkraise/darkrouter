The three dashed returns are the part a status grid cannot show: traffic that arrived somewhere because somewhere else refused it. 41 requests reached cerebras and 96 reached together after groq rate-limited, and 58 reached vertex after openrouter did. They sum to 195 — the 4.0% printed on the router, and the reason together and vertex carry more than their priority alone would earn them.

Cerebras is the row worth reading twice. It is degraded, not failed: one of its three credentials is cooling, and it still carried 1,096 requests. A grid that renders it amber says something is wrong with it; this says something is wrong with one of its keys and the other two are working.
