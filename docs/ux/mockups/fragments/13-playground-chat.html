Chat mode: a conversation the operator can come back to. The history rail on the left retrieves one by its title, the line it last opened with and how long ago; the header strip carries the two settings a conversation actually needs; and under every answer the route line says who served it and how long it took, without turning the transcript into an instrument panel. There is no config pane and no metrics strip here — that is the mode. Press A to reveal pins, T for light mode.

11 · playground-screen.tsx — the mode toggle sits at the right of the page header row, and it is the whole shape of the change: Chat and Lab are two modes, not two tabs among four. Lab keeps the four surfaces (Single, Compare, Auxiliary, Count) inside itself; Chat has no surface strip at all.

1b1b · history-rail.tsx — every conversation in one 260px column, each row carrying the title, one truncated line of the last user turn and a relative timestamp: enough to recognise a conversation without opening it. The rail collapses to nothing, because a rail and a transcript are two columns an operator sometimes wants to be one.

22 · conversation-header.tsx — the model as a bordered pill in mono with the dialect beside it, the title editable in place, and an overflow menu holding the system prompt, open in Lab, and delete. Chat mode shows no config pane, so these two settings are reachable from here instead.

33 · message.tsx — the route line under an answer has two readings. Quiet is Chat mode's default: the provider mark in the gutter and the duration alone, one click from the rest. Expanded is what that click reveals, and what Lab always shows: provider/model, duration, tokens, cost and the trace.
